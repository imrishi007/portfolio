## STATEMENT OF RESEARCH (1000 characters max)

My work focuses on multimodal deep learning for sequential data. I built a system fusing CNNs, BiLSTMs, Graph Attention Networks, and FinBERT to jointly forecast volatility and direction for 30 stocks, achieving Vol R2 of 0.921. Key learnings: gated fusion mechanisms for dynamic modality weighting, QLIKE loss for asymmetric penalties, and skip connections to preserve autoregressive signals lost in neural compression.

Earlier, I trained XGBoost on 76 technical indicators with SMOTE and temporal splits, learning that honest evaluation matters more than inflated metrics. This progression from traditional ML to deep learning taught me how architecture choices encode inductive biases.

I have studied Black Scholes, BERT, PINNs, and diffusion models from IISc Bangalore. I am drawn to fundamental questions: uncertainty quantification, interpretability, and why overparameterized networks generalize. I bring experience building end to end ML systems and genuine curiosity about how neural networks learn.
